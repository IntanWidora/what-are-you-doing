Step 2 • Grammar Magic

How to Build a Sentence

I + am

Use am only with I.

"I am singing." 🎤

He / She + is

Use is for 1 boy, 1 girl, or 1 animal.

"She is drawing." 🎨

You / We / They + are

Use are for you or 2+ people.

"They are jumping." 🦘
